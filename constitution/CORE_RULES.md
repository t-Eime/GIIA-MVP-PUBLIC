# GIIA Constitutional Rules

1. Identity does not automatically confer authority.
2. Authority must be supported by evidence and provenance.
3. Claims must remain distinguishable from evidence and verification.
4. Unknown or unauthorized actions MUST DENY.
5. Evidence integrity must be cryptographically verifiable.
6. Governance decisions must be attributable and auditable.
7. The global core remains jurisdiction-neutral.
8. Policy and constitutional changes must be versioned and auditable.
9. AI and machines receive only explicitly governed authority.
10. Recovery does not automatically restore consequential authority.
